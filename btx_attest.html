BTX — attestation

← order book

Maker attestations let a desk prove control of a bc1p Taproot address by signing a BTX-issued challenge under BIP-322. The desk's wallet signs locally; btxd only verifies. No custodial component — your keys never leave your wallet.

1. Generate a challenge

Click generate for a fresh 32-byte nonce. Send it to a maker over any channel; ask them to BIP-322-sign it under their bc1p maker address (Sparrow, BlueWallet, Magic Eden, Specter, and Saturn all support bc1p BIP-322 signing today). Paste the signature back below.

no challenge yet

2. Verify a maker's signature

Paste the maker's bc1p address, the exact message they signed (usually the challenge from step 1), and their BIP-322 signature string. Both smp... (simple) and ful... (full, with timelocks) formats are supported.

no signature submitted yet

3. Endpoints (CLI reference)

If you want to wire this into a script:

GET  /api/attest/challenge
     → {"challenge_hex":"<64-hex>"}

POST /api/attest/verify
     body: {"address","message","signature"}
     → {"valid":bool,"format":"simple"|"full"}
     Malformed input → 400 with informative error.
