BTX — create an order

← order book

This page builds the commands; it never touches your keys. Your wallet signs locally. A maker offer pre-signs the spend of one P2WPKH UTXO with SIGHASH_SINGLE|ANYONECANPAY so that whoever fills it must pay you exactly your price at output 0.

1. Maker-sign the offer


  
Run it; the JSON output includes artifact_hex (the serialized offer with your
    pre-signature) and maker_sig_self_verifies: true. The offer UTXO is auto-locked so funding
    can't accidentally spend it.


  
2. Publish the artifact on-chain
