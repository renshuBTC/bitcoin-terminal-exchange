BTX

A server-less, on-chain order book for Bitcoin.

No exchange, no relay, no escrow. Orders live on the Bitcoin blockchain; your own node rebuilds the book by reading the chain. Settlement is a single Bitcoin transaction.

View the order book Create an order

How it works

  1. A maker pre-signs a partial transaction with SIGHASH_SINGLE|ANYONECANPAY — locking in "spend my offer UTXO and you must pay me exactly this price at output 0," leaving the rest open.
  2. The signed offer is published on-chain as a compact BTX1 artifact (OP_RETURN or a Taproot witness envelope).
  3. Your node's indexer reads the artifact from the chain, verifies the signature, and lists it as an open order — no server, no off-chain state.
  4. A taker completes the half-signed transaction and broadcasts; the swap settles atomically in one transaction. Spending the offer marks the order filled or cancelled.

What you're looking at

Order book — open orders, partial-fill groups, recent fills/cancels, and detected on-chain atomic swaps, all reconstructed from chain data.

Served by a BTX-fork node over /api/v1/btx/*. Point the pages at your node's API on the order-book screen.

Research preview. Trust-minimized to Bitcoin alone — no token, no middleman, first valid spender wins. Proven on regtest and signet; not production-hardened. The order book is empty until orders are published on-chain.
